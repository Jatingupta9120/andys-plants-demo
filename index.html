Our Story

Family-Run.
Grown, Not Bought In.

Andy's Plants is a small, family-run nursery based in Rednal. We grow all of our own bedding and perennial plants ourselves rather than buying in stock from a wholesaler — so everything on our tables has been raised right here, by hand.

That means our range moves with the seasons: bright bedding and hanging baskets through spring and summer, hardy perennials that come back year after year, and always something new potted up out the back. Come and see what's ready, or send us a message and we'll tell you what's on the tables this week.
